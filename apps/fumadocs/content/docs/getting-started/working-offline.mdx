---
title: Working offline
description: What's saved on your device, and what “to sync” means.
---

Networks in the pilot area can be slow or absent. Ecolens is built to keep working without them.

## What works offline

- **Every screen you've opened before** opens with the data last saved on your device. Lists show when they were last updated.
- **Every action** (a decision, a field report and its photos, an escalation, an assignment, an action taken, a report request) is **saved on your device first** and sent when there's a connection.
- **Map imagery** you've already looked at is kept for a while, so the map works where you've been.

## The connection indicator

| It says | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Live** | Connected, and changes by others appear as they happen |
| **Online** | Connected |
| **Offline** | No connection; you're seeing saved data |
| **Offline · 2 to sync** | Two of your changes are saved on the device, waiting |
| **Syncing 2…** | Sending them now |
| **1 couldn't sync** | The server refused one change. Open the indicator to see why |

Changes are sent **in the order you made them**, as soon as you're back online, when you return to the app, and every 30 seconds while it's open.

## On the screen

Things saved on your device but not yet sent are marked **Saved on this device** or **Not synced yet**. They show the result you asked for (for example the new status) until the server confirms it.

<Callout type="warn">
Don't sign out while changes are waiting to sync, unless you mean to discard them. Ecolens asks you first.
</Callout>

If something couldn't sync, see [sync problems](/docs/troubleshooting/sync).
