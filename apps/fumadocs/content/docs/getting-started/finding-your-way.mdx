## The menu

The menu (on the left on a computer, at the bottom on a phone) shows only the sections your role uses. Each section matches a stage of how Ecolens works:

| Section | Stage | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | | Your role's dashboard |
| **Map** | Interpret | Alerts, rivers, basins, reserves and concessions on one map |
| **Alerts** | Validate | The review queue: evidence, field checks, decisions |
| **Incidents** | Escalate | Confirmed changes routed to an institution, and the response |
| **Field reports** | Observe | Reports and photos from the field; checks to do |
| **Reports** | Report | One-page assessment reports |
| **Audit trail** | | Every recorded change, with who, when and why |
| **Administration** | | Members, roles and data sources (administrators) |

Hiding a section is a convenience; Ecolens also checks your role on every action.

## The top bar

- **Connection and sync** (top right): shows **Live**, **Online**, **Offline**, or how many changes are waiting to sync. Open it to see each change. See [working offline](/docs/getting-started/working-offline).
- **Your initials:** account, theme and **Sign out**.

## Your overview

Each role has its own dashboard. For example:

- **Analysts** see alerts waiting for a decision, by confidence and age, and how each rule's alerts were decided.
- **Field officers** see the checks asked of their team and the reports still on their phone.
- **Responders** see their assembly's cases and what's overdue.
- **Leadership** sees the pilot's [success measures](/docs/guides/leadership/success-measures).

Most numbers on a dashboard are links: select one to open the matching list.

## Shareable views

The address in your browser includes the filters, the open alert and the map view. Copy it to show a colleague exactly what you're looking at. They see only what their own role allows.
