---
title: Signing in
description: Invitation, email code and choosing your institution.
---

Access to Ecolens is **by invitation only**. There is no public sign-up: your institution's administrator invites you with a role.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Accept your invitation

Open the invitation email and follow the link. It brings you to the Ecolens sign-in page.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter your email

Type the email address the invitation was sent to and continue. Ecolens sends you a **6-digit code**. If your institution uses them, you can sign in with **Microsoft** or **Google** instead.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the code

Type the code from the email. If it doesn't arrive, check spam, then use **Resend code**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose your institution

If you belong to more than one institution, choose the one you're acting for. If you belong to one, it's chosen for you. You can switch later from the institution name at the top of the menu.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What you see next

You land on your **overview**: a dashboard chosen for your role and institution. See [finding your way](/docs/getting-started/finding-your-way).

## Signing out on a shared device

Use the menu under your initials (top right) → **Sign out**. Signing out needs a connection. Data saved on the device for offline use is **deleted when you sign out**, so the next person can't see it. If you have changes that haven't synced yet, the menu says so and offers **Sign out and discard them**: only use it if you mean to lose them.

<Callout type="warn">
On a shared phone or office computer, always sign out when you're done.
</Callout>
