---
title: “This changed before your update arrived”
description: When someone else acted first.
---

Ecolens never lets one person's change silently overwrite another's. If an alert or incident changed between when you opened it and when your change arrived (for example a colleague decided first, or you were offline), your change is refused, and you see:

> *This alert changed before your update arrived: it is now "confirmed", set by …. Review it again before deciding.*

**What to do:** select **Review the latest version**. Ecolens reloads the record so you can see what was done, then decide whether anything else is needed. Or **Discard** your change.

Nothing is lost: the other person's change and yours (refused) are both visible to you.
