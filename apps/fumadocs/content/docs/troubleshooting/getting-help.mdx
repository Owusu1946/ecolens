---
title: Getting help
description: Who to ask.
---

- **Your account, role or institution:** your institution's Ecolens administrator.
- **How to do something:** these pages, or **Ask AI** if it's switched on. It answers from these pages only, and has no access to Ecolens data. Don't type names, phone numbers or locations into it.
- **Something looks wrong:** tell your administrator what you were doing, the time, and any message shown (for example under the connection indicator).

To learn how alerts are reviewed in general, see the public page **How alerts are reviewed** (link at the top).
