## "Getting a GPS fix…" for a long time

- Step outside, away from buildings and dense canopy.
- Check location is on for your phone and allowed for your browser.
- If it says **Location access is blocked**, allow it in the browser's site settings, then **Try again**.
- *Low accuracy, still improving* is normal at first; you can still save.

## Photos

- Up to **6 photos** per report.
- Photos are made smaller on your phone before saving, which takes a moment each.
- If a report shows *1 photo waiting to upload*, it will send when the connection is good. Keep Ecolens open or come back to it.
