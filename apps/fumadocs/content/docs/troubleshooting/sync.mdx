## "2 to sync" and it doesn't go down

- Check you're **online** (the indicator says *Offline* otherwise).
- Bring Ecolens to the front: it retries when you return, and every 30 seconds.
- Photos send after their report; on a slow connection they take longer.

## "1 couldn't sync" or "Not sent: …"

The server **refused** that change, and gives the reason. Open the connection indicator (top right) to see each one:

- **Try again**: after fixing the cause (for example, getting the right role).
- **Discard this change**: if it's no longer needed.

Common reasons:

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| *This alert changed before your update arrived* | Someone else acted first. See [conflicts](/docs/troubleshooting/conflicts) |
| *Your role does not allow this action* | Ask your administrator about your role |
| *… not found* | It was removed or isn't available to your role |

## Signing out with changes waiting

Ecolens warns you. Signing out anyway **discards** them.
