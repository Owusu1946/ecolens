---
title: Sensitive locations
description: Who sees exact places, and why.
---

Some places need protection: communities, ongoing investigations, and the exact spots field officers visit.

- **Alerts and incidents marked sensitive** are visible only to roles cleared for sensitive locations (analysts, regulators, field officers, leadership, admins). The map tells other roles how many locations are withheld.
- **Field reports** are sensitive by default: they place people and activity at exact locations.
- **Reports** leave out field coordinates and photos, and reports on sensitive locations can't be circulated.
- **The Ask AI help assistant** has no access to Ecolens data at all.

Showing sensitive locations approximately (for example district-level counts) is awaiting a steering-group decision.
