---
title: The audit trail
description: Why every change is recorded, and how.
---

Every status change in Ecolens (start review, decision, field-check request, escalation, assignment, acknowledgement, action, closure, report, approval, membership change) is recorded with:

- **who**: role and institution,
- **when**,
- **why**: the reason given,
- what it changed **from** and **to**.

The record is **append-only**: the database itself refuses edits and deletions. Mistakes are corrected with a new entry, so the history stays complete.

Roles with access can read it under **Audit trail**, and an independent evaluator can export it. See [the audit trail guide](/docs/guides/leadership/audit-trail).
