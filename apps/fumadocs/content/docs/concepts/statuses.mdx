title: Statuses, confidence and severity
description: What each label means.
---

## Alert status

Colours show **where an alert is in the review**, not how alarming it is.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Needs review** (amber) | New; nobody has picked it up |
| **In review** (orange) | Someone is reviewing it |
| **Confirmed by review** (rose) | The change was verified by a person. Not a finding of responsibility |
| **Unresolved** (violet) | Reviewed; evidence not conclusive. Can be decided later |
| **Rejected by review** (grey) | Not borne out. Hidden on the map by default |

## Incident status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting assignment** | Opened; no institution asked to act yet |
| **Assigned** | An institution has been asked to act |
| **In progress** | The assigned institution has acknowledged it |
| **Action recorded** | At least one action (or decision not to act) is recorded |
| **Closed** | Closed with a summary |

**Overdue** appears when a due date passes while a case is still assigned or in progress.

## Confidence

**Low**, **medium** or **high**, never a percentage. An alert's confidence comes from the rule; a decision carries the reviewer's own confidence.

## Severity (incidents)

The **potential environmental impact**, never anyone's culpability: *high* (serious or spreading harm, or risk to people or water supply), *medium* (clear harm, contained or slow), *low* (limited harm; worth recording).
