---
title: Roles
description: Who does what in Ecolens.
---

Your role comes from your institution's invitation. Each role sees only what its job needs.

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| **Analyst / Reviewer** | Reviews alerts, asks for field checks, records decisions, escalates, generates reports |
| **Regulator** | Everything an analyst does for decisions, plus assigns and closes incidents |
| **Field officer** | Checks alerts on the ground and files field reports |
| **Responder** | Acknowledges and records action on cases assigned to their institution |
| **Leadership** | Oversees success measures, approves reports for circulation |
| **Independent evaluator** | Reads decisions, outcomes and the audit trail; changes nothing |
| **Data steward** | Looks after their institution's data sources |
| **Viewer** | Reads alerts, incidents and reports |
| **Platform admin** | Manages members and roles |

Your **dashboard** depends on your role and institution: a regulator at the EPA, the Minerals Commission and the Water Resources Commission each see their own.

The full list is in [roles and permissions](/docs/reference/permissions).
