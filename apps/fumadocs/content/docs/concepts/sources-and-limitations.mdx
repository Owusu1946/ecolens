title: Sources and limitations
description: Provenance on everything, and what data can't tell you.
---

Every layer, reading and alert in Ecolens shows where it came from:

- **Source** and the **institution** that stewards it,
- **Mode**: **Live** (fetched automatically), **Prepared** (assembled for the pilot) or **Simulated** (demonstration data),
- **Update frequency**, **resolution** and **licence**,
- **Known limitations**.

## Why limitations matter

| Data | It can't tell you |
|---|---|
| Satellite river colour (turbidity proxy) | A laboratory measurement; reads nothing under cloud; rain and haze raise values |
| Canopy loss and bare ground | Who cleared it, or whether it was permitted |
| Thermal detections (VIIRS, 375 m) | Machinery or mining from farm or charcoal fires |
| Low-cost air sensors | Reference-grade values; humidity can inflate readings |
| Concession boundaries (prototype) | The legal cadastre: they're illustrative |

Read the **Known limitations** on every alert before deciding.
