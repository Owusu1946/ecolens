---
title: How Ecolens works
description: From a reading to a filed record, in six stages.
---

| Stage | What happens | Where in Ecolens |
|---|---|---|
| **Observe** | Satellite imagery, river and air readings, agency records and field reports are gathered, each with a place, a time and a source. | Map, Field reports |
| **Analyse** | Agreed, versioned rules compare readings with what's normal for that place, and raise an alert with a confidence level. | (automatic) |
| **Interpret** | The alert is shown with its evidence, sources, coverage and known limitations. | Map, Alerts |
| **Validate** | A person reviews it, asks for a field check if needed, and records a decision with a reason. | Alerts |
| **Escalate** | A confirmed change becomes an incident, routed to the responsible institution, which records what it did. | Incidents |
| **Report** | A one-page record is filed, and the pilot's success is measured. | Reports, Overview |

**An alert is not an incident.** An alert becomes an incident only after human validation.

## The commitments behind it

- **People decide.** Nothing moves forward without a recorded human decision.
- **An alert is not an accusation.** It's a prompt to look more closely.
- **Uncertainty is shown.** Low, medium, high; every source with its limits; prepared data labelled.
- **Everything is on the record.** Who, when and why, and it can't be edited.
- **Sensitive places are protected.** Each role sees only what it needs.
- **Institutions keep authority over their data.**
