---
title: Glossary
description: Terms used in Ecolens.
---

**Alert:** a possible change flagged by an agreed rule; a prompt for review.

**Audit trail:** the append-only record of every change, with who, when and why.

**Basin:** the land draining into a river: the Pra and the Ankobra in the pilot.

**Concession:** an area licensed for mining. Boundaries in the prototype are illustrative.

**Confidence:** low, medium or high: how sure the rule, or the reviewer, is.

**Escalate:** turn a confirmed alert into an incident.

**Field check:** a request for a field team to look at an alert on the ground.

**FRP (fire radiative power):** the heat output of a thermal detection, in megawatts.

**Incident:** a confirmed alert routed to an institution for a response.

**Lead institution:** the institution that owns an incident and its outcome.

**Lens:** water, forests and land, or air.

**Live / prepared / simulated:** whether data is fetched automatically, assembled for the pilot, or made up for the demonstration.

**PM2.5:** fine particles in the air, in micrograms per cubic metre.

**Rule:** an agreed, versioned test that turns readings into an alert.

**Sensitive location:** a place shown only to roles that need it.

**Severity:** potential environmental impact of an incident, never culpability.

**Turbidity:** how cloudy river water is. Ecolens uses a satellite proxy, not a lab measurement.

**VIIRS:** the satellite instrument behind NASA's thermal detections (375 m pixels).
