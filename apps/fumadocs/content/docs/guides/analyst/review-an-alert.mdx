---
title: Review an alert
description: Read the evidence before anyone acts.
---

<Task stage="Validate" roles="Analyst · Regulator" offline time="5 minutes" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the queue

Go to **Alerts**. The **Needs a decision** tab lists alerts that are new, in review or unresolved. Use **Lens**, **Confidence** and **Basin** to narrow it, and **Sort** to see the oldest or highest-confidence first.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open an alert

Select it. On a computer it opens beside the list; on a phone it fills the screen (use **Queue** to go back).

</Step>
<Step>

### Tell colleagues you have it (optional)

Select **Start review**. The alert shows as **In review**, so others know someone is on it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Read everything it rests on

- **Details:** where, when it was first observed and when it was flagged.
- **Known limitations:** what this kind of data can't tell you. Read this every time.
- **Rule that flagged it:** the agreed rule and its version.
- **Evidence:** each reading with its source, the institution responsible, whether it's live, prepared or simulated, its confidence and its limits. Field reports appear here too.
- **Decisions** and **Audit trail:** anything already decided or done.

</Step>
<Step>

### See it in place

Select **View on map** to see the alert with rivers, basins, reserves and concessions.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Principle>
An alert is a prompt for review, not proof of wrongdoing. A satellite can't tell a mining pit from a farm clearing, and haze or rain can affect river readings.
</Principle>
