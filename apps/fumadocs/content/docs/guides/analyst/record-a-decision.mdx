---
title: Record a decision
description: Confirm, reject or leave an alert unresolved, with your reason.
---

<Task stage="Validate" roles="Analyst · Regulator" offline time="2 minutes" />

Nothing moves forward without a person's recorded decision.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the alert and select "Record a decision"

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose an outcome

| Outcome | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Confirm** | The change is real. This is *not* a finding that anyone is responsible. |
| **Reject** | The change isn't borne out: cloud, a sensor fault, seasonal variation, a farm fire. |
| **Unresolved** | The evidence isn't conclusive either way. You can decide later. |

</Step>
<Step>

### Say how confident you are

**Low**, **Medium** or **High**: your confidence in the decision, not the rule's.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give your reason

At least 10 characters: what you checked and what led you to this decision. It's recorded with your role, institution and the time, and can't be edited later.

</Step>
<Step>

### Select "Record decision"

</Step>
</Steps>

<Principle>
Confirmed means the change was verified by people. It is never a finding that any person or company did something wrong.
</Principle>

## After you decide

- **Confirmed** and **Rejected** are final. **Unresolved** can be decided later.
- A confirmed alert can be [escalated to an incident](/docs/guides/analyst/escalate-an-alert).
- Rejections count towards each rule's rejection rate, which scientists use to review thresholds.

If someone else decided first, you'll see a notice instead. See [conflicts](/docs/troubleshooting/conflicts).
