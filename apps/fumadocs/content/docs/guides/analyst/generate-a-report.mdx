A report records the case **as it stands at that moment**: evidence, confidence, the decision, the responsible institution and the action taken, with a map. Once filed it never changes; generate a new one later if the case moves on.

<Tabs items={["From an incident", "From an alert"]}>
<Tab value="From an incident">

Open the incident and, under **Reports**, select **Generate assessment report**.

</Tab>
<Tab value="From an alert">

For an alert that wasn't escalated (for example, one that was rejected), open it and, under **Reports**, select **Generate alert summary**.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Then open it from **Reports**, or from the link in the confirmation message.

## Reading, printing and sharing

- **PDF** downloads the filed PDF. If PDFs aren't available yet, use **Print** and choose *Save as PDF*: it's the same page.
- Field reports appear **without exact locations or photos**, because report readers may not be cleared for them.
- Reports on prepared data carry a **Prototype · simulated data** banner.
- Reports on sensitive locations carry a **Sensitive** banner and can't be approved for circulation.

Circulating a report to partner institutions needs [approval](/docs/guides/leadership/approve-a-report).
