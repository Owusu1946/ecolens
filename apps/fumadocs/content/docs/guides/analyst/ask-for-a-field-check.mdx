---
title: Ask for a field check
description: Have a field team confirm an alert on the ground.
---

<Task stage="Validate" roles="Analyst · Regulator" offline time="2 minutes" />

When the evidence isn't enough to decide, ask an institution's field team to look on the ground.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the alert

From **Alerts**, open an alert that **needs a decision**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Select "Ask for a field check"

Choose the **institution** whose field team should go, and write **what to check**: where to go, what to look for, what to photograph.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a date if it's urgent

**Needed by** is optional. Overdue checks are highlighted for the field team.

</Step>
<Step>

### Send

Select **Ask for a field check**. It appears under **Field checks** as *waiting for a field report*.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

- The field team sees it in **Field reports → To check**, nearest first.
- When one of them files a report on this alert, the check shows **answered**, and the report appears in the **Evidence**.
- If the alert is **confirmed or rejected** before anyone goes, open checks are **withdrawn** automatically, so nobody travels for nothing.

The alert's status doesn't change when you ask for a check.
