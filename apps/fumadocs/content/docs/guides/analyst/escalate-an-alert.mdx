---
title: Escalate an alert
description: Turn a confirmed alert into an incident.
---

<Task stage="Escalate" roles="Analyst · Regulator" offline time="2 minutes" />

Only a **confirmed** alert can be escalated, and only once.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the confirmed alert and select "Escalate to an incident"

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in the incident

- **Title:** starts as the alert's title; edit it if needed.
- **Severity:** the potential environmental impact, **not** anyone's culpability.
  - *High:* serious or spreading harm, or risk to people or water supply.
  - *Medium:* clear harm that is contained or slow-moving.
  - *Low:* limited harm; worth recording and watching.
- **Lead institution:** owns the case and its outcome. Others can be assigned to act later.
- **Why escalate:** why this confirmed change needs an institutional response.

</Step>
<Step>

### Select "Open incident"

</Step>
</Steps>

The incident opens as **Awaiting assignment**. A regulator then [assigns it](/docs/guides/regulator/escalate-and-assign).
