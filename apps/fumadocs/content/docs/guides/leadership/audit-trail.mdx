Go to **Audit trail**. Every status change and decision is listed with:

- **what** happened (for example *Alert decided*, *Incident assigned*, *Role changed*),
- **who**: their role and institution (automated analysis is labelled as such),
- **when**, and **why**: the reason they gave.

Filter by **record** (alerts, incidents, field reports, reports, membership), **role** and **dates**. Select an entry to open the record.

## Export

**Export CSV** downloads up to 5,000 entries matching your filters, for independent evaluation of detection performance, response speed and unintended effects.

The audit trail **can't be edited or deleted**. Corrections are added as new entries.
