---
title: Approve a report for circulation
description: Release a report to partner institutions.
---

<Task stage="Report" roles="Leadership · Admin" offline time="1 minute" />

Approval releases a report to **partner institutions signed in to Ecolens**. Nothing is published publicly until the steering group agrees publication rules.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the report from "Reports"

</Step>
<Step>

### Select "Approve for circulation"

</Step>
<Step>

### Say why it can be circulated

For example: *Reviewed with the lead institution; no sensitive locations.* The note is recorded in the audit trail.

</Step>
</Steps>

Reports on **sensitive locations** can't be approved for circulation.

## Tell us if it helped

Under each report: **Did this report help a decision?** → **Useful** or **Not useful**, with an optional comment. You can change your answer later. This feeds the *decision usefulness* measure.
