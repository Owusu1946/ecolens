## 1. Escalate the confirmed alert

See [escalate an alert](/docs/guides/analyst/escalate-an-alert). The incident opens as **Awaiting assignment**.

## 2. Assign it

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the incident

From **Incidents → Awaiting assignment**, or from the alert's **Incident** section.

</Step>
<Step>

### Select "Assign"

Choose the **institution** that should act (for example the district assembly), write **instructions** (what to do and what to report back), and optionally a **due by** date.

</Step>
<Step>

### Select "Assign"

The incident becomes **Assigned**. The institution sees it in its own list.

</Step>
</Steps>

You can assign more than one institution: select **Assign another institution**. The lead institution stays responsible for the case.

## Following the response

The register shows **Overdue** when a due date passes before an action is recorded. On the incident you'll see each assignment, each action, the audit trail and the time from validation to first action.
