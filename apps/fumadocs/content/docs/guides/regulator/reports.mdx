---

<Task stage="Report" roles="Regulator · Analyst" />

- **Generate** a report from an incident or an alert: see [generate a report](/docs/guides/analyst/generate-a-report).
- **Find** reports under **Reports**: filter by *Approved for circulation* or *Not yet approved*.
- **Approval** for circulation to partner institutions is done by leadership: see [approve a report](/docs/guides/leadership/approve-a-report).

Nothing is published publicly from Ecolens until the steering group agrees publication rules.
