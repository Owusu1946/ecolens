---
title: Close an incident
description: Close a case with a summary of the outcome.
---

<Task stage="Escalate" roles="Regulator" offline time="2 minutes" />

An incident can be closed once **at least one action is recorded**. A recorded *decision not to act* counts.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the incident (status "Action recorded")

</Step>
<Step>

### Select "Close incident"

Write a **closing summary**: the outcome, and anything to follow up. It appears on the incident and in reports.

</Step>
<Step>

### Select "Close incident"

</Step>
</Steps>

Then [generate an assessment report](/docs/guides/analyst/generate-a-report) to file the record.
