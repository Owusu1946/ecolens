<Task stage="Admin" roles="Platform admin" />

Go to **Administration → Members**. Each institution is listed with its members and pending invitations. Institutions themselves are set up by the platform team, not here.

<Callout type="warn">
These actions need a connection: they aren't saved for later like workflow actions.
</Callout>

## Invite someone

1. On the institution, select **Invite**.
2. Enter their **email** and choose a **role**. Give the least access the job needs.
3. Select **Send invitation**. They get an email to join.

Pending invitations can be withdrawn with **Withdraw**.

## Change a role

Select **Change role**, choose the new role and give a **reason**. You can't change your own role.

## Remove access

Select the remove icon and give a **reason**. You can't remove yourself.

Every invitation, role change and removal is recorded in the audit trail. See [roles and permissions](/docs/reference/permissions).
