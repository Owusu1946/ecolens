<Task stage="Escalate" roles="Responder" offline />

Go to **Incidents**. You see **only** the cases your institution leads or has been assigned. Others are not shown to you.

Each case shows its status, severity, lead institution, place and any **Overdue** date.

## Open a case

You'll see:

- **From alert:** what was detected and its limitations.
- **Assignments:** the instructions given to your institution, and the due date.
- **Actions taken** so far.

## Acknowledge it

Select **Acknowledge** to show you've received the case. The status becomes **In progress**.

Then [record what you do](/docs/guides/responder/record-an-action).

<Principle>
An incident records an institutional response to a change confirmed by people. It is not a finding of wrongdoing against any party.
</Principle>
