---
title: Record an action
description: What was done, or a decision not to act.
---

<Task stage="Escalate" roles="Responder" offline time="2 minutes" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the case and select "Record an action"

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose what was done

For example *Site inspection*, *Sampling*, *Enforcement notice*, *Remediation*, *Referral to another institution*, or **Decision not to act**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Describe it

What was found or done, by whom, and anything still outstanding. If you decided not to act, say why: that's a valid, recorded response.

</Step>
<Step>

### Say when, then select "Record action"

</Step>
</Steps>

The status becomes **Action recorded**. You can record more actions as the case continues. A regulator closes the case.
