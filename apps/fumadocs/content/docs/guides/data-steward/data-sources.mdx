Each institution keeps authority over its own data. Every source in Ecolens records its **steward**, whether it's **live**, **prepared** or **simulated**, how often it updates, its resolution, its licence and its **known limitations**.

## Data sources

The list of sources (on the analyst dashboard, and under **Administration → Data sources** for admins) shows each source's steward, update frequency, mode and when its latest reading was.

## Live thermal feed (NASA FIRMS)

Ecolens fetches VIIRS thermal detections for the pilot area **every hour**, and runs the agreed rule *Thermal anomaly near a river* on them. The **Live thermal feed** card on your dashboard shows whether the feed is **on**, how many detections are stored, and when it last fetched. **Fetch now** runs it immediately.

Alerts it opens start at **low confidence**: VIIRS can't tell machinery or mining from farm or charcoal fires.

If the card says **Off**, the feed hasn't been configured yet. Ask your Ecolens administrator.
