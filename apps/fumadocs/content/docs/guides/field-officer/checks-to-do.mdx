<Task stage="Validate" roles="Field officer" offline />

Go to **Field reports → To check**. Each check shows:

- the alert it's about, and where (site and basin),
- what to check, written by the analyst who asked,
- who asked and when, and a due date if there is one (**Overdue** is highlighted).

## Nearest first

Select **Sort by distance**. Your phone asks for your location once; the list then shows each check's distance and puts the nearest at the top.

## Next

- **Report on this** opens a report already linked to the alert.
- **Map** shows where it is.

After you report, the check stays listed as *Report saved on this device* until it syncs, then disappears from your list.
