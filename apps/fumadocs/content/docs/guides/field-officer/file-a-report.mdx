---
title: File a field report
description: GPS, what you saw, and photos.
---

<Task stage="Observe" roles="Field officer" offline time="3 minutes" />

<Steps>
<Step>

### Start the report

From a check, select **Report on this**. To report something without a check, select **New report**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Wait for the GPS fix

The **Location** box shows your coordinates and their accuracy (for example *±8 m*). GPS works **without signal**. In the open it usually takes under a minute. The best reading while the form is open is kept; *low accuracy* means it's still improving.

</Step>
<Step>

### Say whether it supports the alert

If the report is about an alert: **Supports the alert**, **Contradicts the alert** (not there, or another cause) or **Inconclusive** (couldn't tell, or couldn't reach the spot).

</Step>
<Step>

### Describe what you saw

At least 10 characters: water colour, machinery, clearing, people present. **Describe what you observed, not who is responsible.**

</Step>
<Step>

### Tap what was present (optional)

For example *Dredging*, *Sediment plume*, *Forest clearing*, *Nothing observed*.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add photos (up to 6)

**Add photo** opens the camera. Photos are made smaller on your phone, and the camera's own location tag is removed.

</Step>
<Step>

### Select "Save report"

It's saved on your phone straight away, and sent with its photos when there's a connection.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Principle>
A field report is one person's observation at one moment. It informs the decision; it is not on its own proof of wrongdoing.
</Principle>

## Who sees your report

Field reports place people and activity at exact locations, so they're visible only to roles cleared for sensitive locations. Reports on an alert appear in that alert's evidence.
