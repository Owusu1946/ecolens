---
title: Working without signal
description: Reporting from sites with no network.
---

<Task stage="Observe" roles="Field officer" offline />

Most sites have poor or no signal. You can do the whole job offline, **as long as you opened Ecolens once while connected** after signing in.

**Before you leave:**

1. Open **Field reports → To check** while connected, so your checks are saved on the phone.
2. Open the alerts you'll visit, if you want their details with you.

**At the site:**

1. Report as usual. GPS doesn't need signal.
2. The top bar shows **Offline · 1 to sync**. Your report and photos are safe on the phone.

**Back in signal:**

Open Ecolens. Reports send in order (report first, then each photo). Under **My reports**, items move from *Saved on this device* to the list. The top bar returns to **Live** or **Online**.

<Callout type="warn">
Don't sign out or clear the browser's data while reports are waiting to sync.
</Callout>

If a report shows *Not sent*, see [sync problems](/docs/troubleshooting/sync).
