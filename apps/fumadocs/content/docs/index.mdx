Ecolens brings satellite imagery, river and air readings, agency records and field observations onto one shared map. It flags unusual changes with agreed rules, and takes each one through a recorded human review to a documented response.

**It supports your judgement; it does not replace it.** An alert is a prompt for review, never proof that anyone did something wrong.
